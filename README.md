# Brain-Biopsy-needle
---Project Overview

This project focuses on the CAD design and development of a brain biopsy needle for minimally invasive tissue sampling. The work includes geometric design, material selection, and design modifications to explore improved mechanical performance during needle insertion.

---Objectives

1. Develop a 3D CAD model of a brain biopsy needle using SolidWorks.

2. Study Nitinol as a candidate material based on its superelasticity and shape memory properties.

3. Explore design modifications aimed at reducing mechanical tissue damage during insertion.

4. Evaluate structural behavior using preliminary finite element analysis (FEA).

---Design Specifications

1. Overall length: 100 mm

2. Outer diameter: 2 mm

3. Inner diameter: 1.5 mm

Selected candidate material: Nitinol

---Material Selection

Nitinol was considered because of its superelastic behavior, shape memory properties, and ability to undergo substantial deformation under appropriate conditions. Its suitability for this application requires further evaluation of alloy composition, transformation temperatures, mechanical properties, biocompatibility, and sterilization requirements.

---Project Status and Limitations

This is an engineering design project. Any simulation results are dependent on the selected material model, loading conditions, contact definitions, and boundary conditions. Further validation and experimental testing are required before clinical use.
